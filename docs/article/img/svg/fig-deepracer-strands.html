One stock AWS DeepRacer, every kind of Strands
where each piece of the Strands stack touches the car
Camera: Strands Deciderevery frame is judged before a hop: is a person in view, what is ahead, calibrated probabilities. The look tool asks Amazon Nova Pro what it sees.
Drive: strands-robotsthe MobileBaseRobot driver turns a hop into a 20 Hz servo stream over rosbridge; the command gate allow-lists the topics and audits every publish; a trailing zero ends each hop.
Servos and ESC: the deadmanour node on the car zeroes throttle and steering 300 ms after the last heartbeat and turns the tail LED red. Physics stops the car.
Compute: Strands Harnessthe race engineer reaches ROS 2 over SSH under a Cedar policy: reads calibration, remembers the offset, cannot write without the operator.
The loop: Strands Agentsone agent on Amazon Bedrock plans, drives through gated tools, narrates, remembers your name and reports its own metrics.
Voice: Strands Bidipush-to-talk through Amazon Nova Sonic; the word "stop" spoken over the rover reaches the servos as a barge-in.
The process: Strands Boxthe agent runs inside a sandbox whose egress is the car and Bedrock only, with a log line for everything else.
Physics stops the car; the decider explains.
