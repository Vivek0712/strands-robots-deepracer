# Outsider peer review

**(1) Strongest: D.** The only response I could follow start to finish without knowing the field, and it names what nobody else does: the cloud is the single point of failure, and the show must still work with the internet dead. Its four-beat shape (opener, one motion, two refusals, payoff) is one story. C is second for being buildable, but reads as a work schedule.

**(2) Biggest blind spot: B.** It never describes what a person standing at the booth sees. Flywheels, datasets and "ROS for agents" matter to the builder in month three; the audience in the room is absent. A shares a milder version: it cuts U24 ("will I fit between the chairs") as decoration when it is the one motion beat a layperson instantly understands.

**(3) What all five missed:** the sceptic in the audience. Every time the car stops or refuses, someone will assume a person with a joystick or a script did it. Nobody plans the proof that nobody is driving: hand the volunteer the only controls, show empty hands, let a stranger pick the command. Without that, every "it refused" reads as theatre.
