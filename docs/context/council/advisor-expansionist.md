# Expansionist

The pool is pricing this as a demo. It is a platform launch wearing a demo costume.

**Undersold candidates.** U19 (a 2B model polices a frontier model) is the most portable idea in the pool and is buried as "DA8, rover stationary". Every developer in that hall runs an agent with shell access; none has a 150 ms local policy engine. Ship `DeciderGate` as a standalone Strands intervention handler and the robot becomes the memorable example of a thing they install Monday. U10 (same mind, two bodies) is the Strands thesis itself: agent is body-independent, robot is a registry entry. Twin plus DeepRacer plus Yahboom is not a stunt; it is the "ROS for agents" claim made visible. U15 and U17 (deadman node, marshal in the pen) are filed as infrastructure. They are the upstream contribution with the longest half-life: every mobile base in strands-robots needs a fail-closed heartbeat, and nobody has written it.

**The adjacent opportunity nobody names.** The DeepRacer League is over and thousands of these cars sit in closets. A `DeepRacerDriver` registry entry plus a Jazzy image plus this exact stack turns dead inventory into the reference Physical AI dev kit for Strands. "Bring your DeepRacer" is a workshop that scales to every AWS user group on earth, and the builder owns it.

**Second flywheel.** The booth is a labelling farm. Every "fool the decider" frame, every shouted command verdict, every consent-tagged interaction feeds decider-lab. Release a calibrated booth-floor decider and the dataset two weeks after the conference; the demo keeps publishing after the lights go off.

**Biggest shape.** Not twelve beats, one talk. One repo, one beat per directory, one blog per beat, one workshop, three upstream PRs (driver, deadman, decider image gate), one dataset. The stage slot is the trailer. The series is the product.
